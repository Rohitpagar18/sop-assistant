# SOP: Escalation Matrix
## Purpose
Define the correct contacts and paths for escalating IT and system issues.
## When to Use
When standard troubleshooting steps in other SOPs fail, or when a high-severity incident occurs.
## Steps
1. Identify the affected system and the severity of the issue (P1, P2, or P3).
2. Check the current on-call schedule in the "DemoPortal" dashboard.
3. For L1 issues, assign the ticket to the standard support queue.
4. For L2 issues, assign the ticket to the specific technical team (e.g., Network, Database, IAM).
5. For P1 incidents, immediately trigger the P1 bridge call and notify the IT Director.
6. Document the escalation path taken in the ticket comments.
## Escalation
If the L2 team does not respond within 30 minutes, escalate to the IT Manager at ext. 0001 (fake).
## Notes
- The escalation matrix is reviewed and updated every quarter.
- Never escalate a P3 issue directly to a P1 on-call engineer.
